How these statistics are calculated

Distinct counts are capped at 1,024 per column. Numeric bounds preserve source precision. Means are rounded. Numbers beyond 1,024 digits or exponent magnitude 1,024 are excluded from numeric statistics and reported as numeric_skipped in the API. Nested values are profiled as objects or arrays.
