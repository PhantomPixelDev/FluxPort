Working with large files

JSON arrays, CSV and NDJSON stream to disk. Browse indexed pages without loading the entire dataset into the browser. Search scans the dataset and may take longer. YAML, XML and TOML documents use the configured {{bytes .MaxBytes}} limit.

Large conversions are read-only and downloadable for 24 hours. Keep original files and download results you want to keep.
